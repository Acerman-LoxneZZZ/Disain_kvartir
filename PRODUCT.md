# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite and authored CSS. The user delegated the new-project stack and named React and TypeScript as suitable.

## Users
Russian-speaking owners of premium apartments and country houses considering a private interior designer.

## Product Purpose
Show the quality and process of interior design and help visitors begin a discussion about their property.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Responsive single-page site; project galleries with task, area and scope; keyboard/touch/mouse before-after; services, process, designer, FAQ and validated enquiry form. No fake submission success. Name, contacts, projects and services share one configuration file. SEO, reduced motion and accessible navigation are required.

## Brand Commitments
Architectural magazine: warm milk #F4F0E8, graphite #252422, terracotta #A75B43; Cyrillic serif headings and sans-serif text; asymmetry, large photography and generous space.

## Evidence on Hand
The user supplied no real name, contacts, portfolio or portrait. Illustrative content is visibly labelled; no awards, testimonials or invented achievements. Stock interiors use the Unsplash License. An illustrative AI portrait and before-state may be used with disclosure. The form endpoint is unset.

## Product Principles
Let interior imagery lead. Make the scope of work concrete. Keep the path to an enquiry short. Distinguish demonstration from real evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, labelled form fields and errors, dialog focus handling, touch-sized controls, responsive text and prefers-reduced-motion.
